Concepts which I was thinking about regarding water supplies to secluded areas.

1. Community based humidity collector systems with water purification and remineralization built in. The basic idea is to
use the dew point of the air to get water vaper condensate on a substrate (e.g copper needle type) then collect the droplets into a chamber pererably something which is not damaged by water and heat over.
For the dew temperature creation peltier devices can be used because of the structure material longevity.
Currently this technology efficiency is hovering around 5-10% which is not ideal but with adding more solar panels we can offset this extra energy need.
Basicly the cycle of this system would be to use the peltier devices in daytime to extract the humidity and using the wasteheat to heat up a chamber full of distilled water (this can be further looked at what would be the best heat sink material)
and at night with copper needles would transfer this heat out bit by bit creating updraft in an outer shell which will draw colder air from opening at the bottom so the colder air can circulate up to make the condensation start again now with the wasteheat which was captured during the day.
This is the basic idea for now.
